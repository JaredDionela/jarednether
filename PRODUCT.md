# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers looking for data systems experts and full-stack developers.

## Product Purpose
To showcase technical depth, reliability, and past client work, ultimately driving recruiters to view the resume and initiate contact.

## Brand Personality
Professional, precise, reliable, and modern tech aesthetic. Focuses on data systems expertise with a clean, high-signal presentation.

## Anti-references
- Overly flashy Web3 aesthetics.
- Generic SaaS dashboard templates (must not look like a boilerplate dashboard).
- Dark mode only (must support light mode well and clearly).

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Make it easy for hiring managers to find the resume and contact info.
- **Expert precision**: The design should reflect the reliability and structure of a well-architected data system.
- **Modern but grounded**: Draw from Vercel/Linear aesthetics (clean lines, high contrast, intentional typography) without losing the personal portfolio touch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strictly adhere to WCAG AA standards.
- Standard accessibility best practices (good contrast, keyboard navigable).
- Must include reduced motion alternatives (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
